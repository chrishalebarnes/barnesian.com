Apparently new to Skype 5.8, there is a Skype logo hanging out in the upper right corner of every video call you place. Unfortunately the option to turn off the logo is a bit buried. If you just updated and are wondering how to remove the watermark read on!

Open Tools>Options…

* Click the “Advanced tab” in the left pane
* Click on the “Advanced settings” sub-tab in the left pane
* Uncheck the “Show Skype watermark during calls” checkbox
* Click “Save”

{% include post_image.md name="SkypeOptions.png" %}

There you go! That wasn’t so bad was it?
